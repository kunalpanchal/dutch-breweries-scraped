Take a nostalgic trip back to sunny days with Whoop The Orange Scoop – an Orange Ice Cream Milk Shake NEIPA inspired by the classic Dutch 'Split' ice cream! At a smooth 6.2% Vol. ABV, this playful and hazy brew captures the ultimate blend of bright, juicy fruit and velvety creaminess. Crafted with a soft base of barley malt, oats, wheat, and lactose, and loaded with real orange juice, this milk shake NEIPA delivers a luscious flavor profile that is wonderfully sweet and malty, balanced with a gentle touch of sourness and low bitterness. Let this vibrant, dessert-style NEIPA transport you straight to childhood memories with every sip!
